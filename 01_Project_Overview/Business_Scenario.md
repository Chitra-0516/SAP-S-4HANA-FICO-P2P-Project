# Business Scenario – Procure-to-Pay

## Company Background
ABC Manufacturing Pvt. Ltd. is a manufacturing company that purchases raw materials from external vendors.

## Business Requirement
The company needs raw materials for its production process.

## Process Flow

1. Purchase department identifies material requirements.
2. Purchase Requisition is created.
3. Purchase Order is issued to the vendor.
4. Vendor delivers the materials.
5. Goods Receipt is recorded in SAP.
6. Vendor invoice is received.
7. Invoice is verified and posted.
8. Vendor payment is processed by the finance department.

## SAP Integration

SAP MM handles procurement activities.

SAP FI handles accounting entries, vendor liabilities and payments.

## Project Goal
To understand how procurement activities integrate with financial accounting in SAP S/4HANA 2023.
