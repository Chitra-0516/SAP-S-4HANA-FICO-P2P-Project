# Project Scope – SAP S/4HANA 2023 P2P

## 1. Project Name
End-to-End Procure-to-Pay (P2P) Process.

## 2. Business Scenario
A company needs to purchase raw materials from an external vendor.

The process starts with a purchase requirement and ends with vendor payment.

## 3. Project Objectives
- Understand the complete P2P cycle.
- Create and understand vendor master data.
- Understand Purchase Order processing.
- Perform Goods Receipt.
- Understand Invoice Verification.
- Process Vendor Payments.
- Understand FI-MM integration.

## 4. SAP Modules
- SAP MM – Materials Management
- SAP FI – Financial Accounting

## 5. Expected Outcome
Successfully understand and execute the end-to-end P2P process in SAP S/4HANA 2023
