# Organizational Structure – SAP S/4HANA

## Company Details

Company Name: ABC Manufacturing Pvt. Ltd.
Industry: Manufacturing
SAP System: SAP S/4HANA 2023

## Organizational Units

### 1. Client
A client is the highest organizational level in SAP.

### 2. Company Code
A company code is the smallest organizational unit for which complete financial statements can be created.

Proposed Company Code: ABC1
Description: ABC Manufacturing India

### 3. Plant
A plant represents a location where materials are produced or stored.

Proposed Plant: ABC1

### 4. Purchasing Organization
A purchasing organization is responsible for procurement activities.

Proposed Purchasing Organization: PABC

## Project Objective
To understand how organizational units support the Procure-to-Pay process in SAP S/4HANA.
